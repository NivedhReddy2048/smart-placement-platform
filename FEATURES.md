# ✨ Platform Features

The Smart Placement Platform is packed with production-ready features designed for high engagement and data-driven results.

## 🛂 1. Advanced RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)
- **Granular Permissions**: Students can only access career tools; Recruiters can only access hiring pipelines.
- **Data Isolation**: Multi-tenant-like isolation ensures recruiters cannot see jobs or candidates outside their organization.
- **Authenticated APIs**: Every endpoint is protected via JWT with strict ownership checks.

## 🎯 2. AI-Powered Job Matching
- **Match Score Engine**: Calculates a percentage score by mapping student skills to job role requirements.
- **Strategic Gap Analysis**: Identifies exactly which skills are missing for a specific role and projects how adding them will improve the student's "Shortlisting Probability".
- **Dynamic Recommendations**: Provides a "Best Next Move" nudge based on market demand and ATS safety thresholds.

## 📊 3. Recruiter Intelligence Dashboard
- **Aggregate Analytics**: Real-time counters for active jobs, total applications, and hiring pipeline movement.
- **Candidate Pipeline**: Visual tracking of candidate progress from "Applied" to "Hired".
- **Match Index**: Sort and filter candidates by their AI-calculated match score.

## 📄 4. Professional Profile & Resume Logic
- **Onboarding Flow**: Guided experience to collect technical nodes and career goals.
- **Skill Persistence**: Manual and automated skill tracking with proficiency levels.
- **PDF/Docx Support**: Ready for integration with deep-learning resume parsers.

## 🌙 5. "Dark Premium" Design System
- **State-of-the-Art UX**: Glassmorphism, smooth transitions, and high-contrast typography.
- **Responsive Layouts**: Fully mobile-optimized for students on the go.
- **Micro-interactions**: Hover effects and project simulations make the platform feel alive.
